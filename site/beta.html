What does it cost?

Nothing, ever, for the first 10 beta users. You use FlyerBox free for life in exchange for your feedback. For everyone after that, we'll announce simple, flat pricing before the beta ends, and you'll never be charged without notice.

Which MLS does it work with?

FlexMLS. Autofill pulls the details straight from your board's feed after you approve the connection.

Do I need a new sign?

No. We provide 5 yard signs free of charge to beta users.
