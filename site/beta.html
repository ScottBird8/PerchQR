Questions

Quick answers.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the private beta. If we ever add pricing, you'll hear about it before anything changes.

Which MLS does it work with?

Flexmls, which is what UtahRealEstate.com uses. Autofill pulls the details straight from your board's feed after you approve the connection.

Do I need new signs?

No. You print the QR code and put it on the sign you already use.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email support@flyerbox.net and a real person will answer.
